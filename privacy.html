Privacy policy

Chief of staff. Last updated 2026-11-04.

Chief of staff is a personal assistant app. It is not offered to the public: only its owner signs in to it, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in, the app asks Google for access to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses that access only to do what the owner asks: read and search mail, save drafts, send mail the owner has approved, and read and change calendar events.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the owner's own computer, and its sign-in tokens are stored there. To carry out the owner's requests, mail and calendar content is passed to an AI assistant the owner uses on that computer (Claude, made by Anthropic), under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. The app does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Google user data

Chief of staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
